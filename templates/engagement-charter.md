Attach the scope register, responsibility matrix, evidence request schedule, access approval and sampled control plan. Record the expected project population independently of what the collector can see. Identify directory, Kubernetes, database-native, recovery and application evidence owners.

The collection scripts do not remediate, enable APIs, grant roles, exercise failover or invoke workloads. New active tests and changes require a separately agreed plan. The organization remains responsible for adopting requirements and authorizing access.

Technical basis: [Google shared responsibility](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/security/shared-responsibility-shared-fate), [resource hierarchy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/cloud-platform-resource-hierarchy). Assessment planning basis: [NIST SP 800-53A](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/a/r5/final). This charter format is consultant-authored.
