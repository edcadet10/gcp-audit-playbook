**Condition:** What was observed? Distinguish raw evidence from inference.

**Criterion:** Identify the adopted requirement, exact applicable wording or approved standard version, and direct Google documentation explaining the product behavior. Do not invent a provider mandate.

**Evidence:** Evidence IDs, file hashes, relevant JSON fields, scope, time window, population and sample. Record collection errors and limits.

**Cause:** Supported explanation; if unconfirmed, label it a hypothesis.

**Impact:** Credible business consequence and exposure path. Do not imply exploitation occurred without evidence.

**Contrary test:** What would disprove the finding? What was checked? What changed?

**Recommendation:** Intended control outcome, proposed change, dependencies, owner, interim control and rollback considerations. Scripts collect evidence and do not implement it.

**Management response:** Agreed facts, disputed facts, planned action, due date and accountable approver.

**Closure test:** Repeatable evidence required to establish correction and operating effectiveness; independent reviewer and date.

**Residual risk or exception:** Approval, scope, compensating evidence, expiry and review trigger.

Method basis: [NIST SP 800-53A](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/a/r5/final). Product interpretation must use the corresponding [Google control references](../controls/CONTROL-CATALOG.md).
